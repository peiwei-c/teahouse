Tea House Privacy Policy

A calm matching solitaire for playing at home.

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This page explains what Tea House does with information on your phone or tablet. The game is made by Peiwei Chua. You can write to chuapeiwei@gmail.com if something here is unclear.

What stays on your device

Tea House does not ask you to make an account. It does not take your name, email, photos, contacts, or precise location.

The game keeps a small save on the device so your table is still there when you come back:

  • the level you have reached
  • sound on or off
  • bigger tiles on or off
  • whether matching tiles glow

That save never leaves the device. Uninstalling Tea House deletes it.

Ads

Tea House is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner while you play, and a full-screen ad after some cleared tables.

Google may collect information so ads can be shown, measured, and (where allowed) personalized. That can include:

  • your device’s advertising ID
  • IP address, which can suggest a general area
  • device and app information
  • whether an ad was shown or tapped

Google and its advertising partners may share that information. Tea House does not sell your information. Ads are requested with a family-friendly content rating (PG).

Read Google’s policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data in apps is explained here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Internet permission

The app uses the internet to load ads. The matching table itself is played on your device.

Children

Tea House is made for adults playing at home. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalized ads in your Google account or device ad settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID in Android or iOS settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove the local save.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. Keep using Tea House after an update means you accept the new page.
